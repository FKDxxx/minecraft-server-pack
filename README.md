# minecraft-server-pack

Resource packs for our Minecraft 26.3 server. Players get them automatically when they join (nothing to install).

## `silent-droppers.zip` (server.properties pack)

- **Clean glass** - glass, stained glass and tinted glass have no border frame, so blocks placed side by side look like one clean surface.
- **Silent droppers, dispensers and minecarts.**

## `shading-pack.zip` (sent by the server's ServerPacks plugin, at the bottom of the other packs)

- **Soft corner shading** on grass, flowers, saplings, crops, torches, ladders, vines, redstone, fire, coral and more
  (vanilla leaves these flat). Every other pack's own models still win.

## `sky-pack.zip` (sent by the server's ServerPacks plugin, on top of the other packs)

- **Waving leaves** - bushy leaves that sway in the wind (natural trees; leaves you place keep their normal look).
- **Waving grass, flowers and vines.**
- **Living water** - a rolling swell, ripples and sky reflection, in every biome's water (swamps too).
- **Soft clouds** - soft, drifting clouds in the sky instead of the blocky layer; warm at sunset, heavy in rain.
- **Sky** - deeper blue overhead, warm sunset band.
- **Light that follows the game's light** - cool shade wherever the sky is blocked (under trees, roofs, overhangs),
  warm sunlit surfaces, and torch light in round, soft pools that fade from warm white to an orange glow into shadow.

Works in plain vanilla 26.3 through core shaders and model tweaks only, and looks the same with the Sodium
performance mod (the pack also carries Sodium versions of the block shaders).
